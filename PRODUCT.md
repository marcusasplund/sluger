# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Players exploring a browser garden with keyboard, mouse or touch.

## Product Purpose
A stealth adventure at slug level: forage, survive and return to the family pot.

## Brand Personality
Intimate, curious, vulnerable. Tidewater informs environmental density and atmosphere.

## Anti-references
Avoid arbitrary offscreen punishment and interfaces that obscure the garden.

## Design Principles
- Keep the garden and its creatures central.
- Telegraph danger and give players a chance to respond.
- Make family behaviour readable through movement and concise status text.
- Preserve the existing visual language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Preserve keyboard and touch controls, reduced motion settings, and text alongside colour warnings.
